# Customer Support Agent Policy

## Password Reset

Employees can reset their password from the self-service portal. If self-service reset fails, they must contact the IT service desk.

## Account Lockout

An account lockout should be escalated to the IT service desk after 30 minutes or after three failed unlock attempts.

## Payroll FAQ

Payroll cutoff questions should be routed to HR operations. The support agent must not invent payroll dates when the policy page does not provide them.
