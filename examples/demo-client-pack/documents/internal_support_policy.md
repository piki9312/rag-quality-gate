# Internal Support Policy

## Password Reset {#sec-1}

Employees must start with the self-service portal. If self-service password reset still fails they must contact the IT service desk.

## Suspected Security Incident {#sec-2}

If an employee suspects phishing malware or account compromise they must call the Security Operations hotline and open a security incident ticket immediately.

## Paid Leave Eligibility {#sec-3}

Employees become eligible for paid leave after 90 calendar days of employment.

## Remote Work Requests {#sec-4}

Employees must submit occasional remote work requests in the HR portal and receive manager approval before the remote day begins.

## Payroll Guardrail {#sec-5}

If payroll cutoff dates are not listed in this policy the support agent must route the question to Payroll Operations and must not invent payroll dates.
