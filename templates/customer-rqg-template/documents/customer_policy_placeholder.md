# Customer Policy Placeholder

## Password Reset {#sec-1}

Users must try the self-service portal first. If the password reset still fails they must contact the IT support desk.

## Suspected Security Incident {#sec-2}

If a user suspects phishing malware or account compromise they must contact the Security team and open an incident ticket immediately.

## Leave Eligibility {#sec-3}

Employees become eligible for paid leave after 90 calendar days of employment.

## Payroll Guardrail {#sec-4}

If payroll cutoff dates are not listed in the policy the support agent must route the question to Payroll Operations and must not invent payroll dates.
