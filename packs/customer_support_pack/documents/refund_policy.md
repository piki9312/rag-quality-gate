# Refund Policy

## Standard Plan

Customers on the standard monthly plan may request a refund within 14 days of the first payment when the service was not used for production work. Refund requests after 14 days are not eligible unless support confirms a service defect.

## Digital Products

Downloaded digital products are not eligible for refund after the download is completed. Support may review access defects, duplicate charges, or delivery failures, but agents must not promise a refund before review.

## Support Review

Refund support requests should include the order ID, the reason for the request, and any relevant error message. Support review is required before a refund is approved.
