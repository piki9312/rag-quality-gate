# Cancellation Policy

## How To Cancel

Customers can cancel from account settings by opening Billing and selecting the cancellation page. Support can guide customers to the page but should not cancel on behalf of the customer without written authorization.

## Renewal Deadline

To avoid the next renewal charge, cancellation must be completed at least 3 business days before the renewal date. Cancellations submitted after that deadline apply to the following renewal cycle.

## Annual Contract Cancellation

Annual contracts remain active through the paid term. Early cancellation does not create an automatic refund unless the refund policy separately applies.
